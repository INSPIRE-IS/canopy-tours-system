Welcome!
So, you're looking for booking software for your tour & activity business. You're not alone! The #1 question we see posted in the 13,000-member Tourpreneur Facebook Group is: "What booking software should I use for my tour business?" 
And we always respond the same way: It depends.
Are you a multi-day adventure operator? A watersports activity operator with boats? A walking tour business with dozens of guides? Every business comes with its unique needs, and that's why there are 100+ software systems to support you.
Which one you choose depends on your unique needs. But what are your needs? That's the point of this guide: to help you uncover your needs, and learn what's possible with booking software.
This first-of-a-kind guide to software features covers 21 core questions to ask yourself when choosing software. Below each question you'll find 229 more granular features and questions to consider.
With this Tourpreneur guide in hand, you'll learn the possibilities out there, helping you become a more informed customer as you talk with potential software companies.
How to use this guide
Here are some pointers to guide you on your booking software search.
There's no single 'holy grail' piece of software out there for you. Almost  all tour operators use booking software plus other solutions in their 'tech stack'. A lot of tour operators waste a lot of their time continually looking for new software solutions.
Listen to, but don't be swayed by others' recommendations. Every company is different, and what works well for another operator may be a  bad idea for you.
If you're a new business, think about where you're headed. Will you need to schedule guides in the future, or manage vehicles? Do you plan to connect to major OTAs and resellers? Your needs may be simple now, but they'll get complex down the road.
If you're a current business, map out the state of your systems. How do your tours work? What could be more streamlined? What would your team like to see improved?
Use this guide to decide on the must-have and nice-to-have features you need. This guide is a comprehensive list of potential software features. No software will do it all, but use the lists that follow to narrow down the core needs of your business. Be willing to give up 'nice to have' features. 
Schedule company demos once you have your needs in order. Plus, if you join Tourprenur as a Professional Member, you'll have access to our software directory of 400+ companies offering services to tour operators that you can browse to find the perfect candidate.
 
What kinds of tours & activities can the software handle?
What kind of business are you? Your conversation with a software provider should start by understanding that not every booking software handles every kind of tours.
Most importantly, booking software is very different in both features and pricing for day and multi-day tours. It is very common for an operator who offers both kinds of tours to  use different booking systems for each kind of tour.
Start by examining the kinds of tour & activities that you offer, or will offer. Different software handles different kinds of experiences.
